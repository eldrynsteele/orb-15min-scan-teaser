## The concept

Each morning, the first 15 minutes of the regular session (9:30–9:45 ET) set an
opening range. Breakouts from that range — confirmed by a candle CLOSE beyond it,
not just a wick — are the highest-conviction entries of the morning session.

The scan runs at ~9:50 ET: late enough that the range is set and the first
post-range candles have printed, early enough that breakouts are still fresh.

## Manual scan procedure

1. **Mark the range.** On a 1-minute or 5-minute chart, note the high and low
   printed between 9:30 and 9:45 ET. That box is the opening range.
2. **Wait for a close beyond it.** A candle must CLOSE above the range high
   (long setup) or below the range low (short setup). Wicks touching the edge
   do not count.
3. **Confirm with volume + VWAP.** The breakout candle should print on
   above-average volume, and price should be on the right side of the day's
   VWAP (above VWAP for longs, below for shorts).
4. **Grade each ticker:**
   - **ready** — closed beyond the range, volume + VWAP confirm
   - **waiting** — pressing against the range edge but no close yet
   - **rejected** — broke out and failed back inside, or VWAP/volume disagree
   - **already played out** — the move happened; chasing it now is late
5. **Entries use 1:1 risk/reward.** Stop at the range edge (or structure),
   target an equal distance. Nothing is ordered without a preview and an
   explicit yes.
